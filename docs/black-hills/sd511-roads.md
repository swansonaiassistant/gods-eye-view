# South Dakota 511 public road reports

The [Northwest Passage transportation data exchange report](https://nwpassage.info/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/14-2-scope-of-work.pdf), South Dakota section, records a ZIP URL supplied by South Dakota as a public 511 feed: `https://sd.cdn.iteris-atis.com/xml/511.zip`. On 2026-11-19 it returned HTTP 200 and a fresh 10,612-byte ZIP with `511.xml` (207,715 bytes). That live XML had 113 active items and 129 route locations: 95 construction, 17 conditions, 6 closures, 1 event (categories can overlap). The older report described only conditions; the live feed has a broader schema. Its contents can change.

The app fetches the fixed URL through `/api/sd511` with a 12-second timeout, compressed and expanded byte limits, one-entry ZIP validation, 60-second memory cache, and at most 30 minutes of explicitly stale cached response on upstream failure. It does not persist or bundle the feed. The opt-in layer polls every five minutes and supports manual refresh. Unknown or malformed locations are omitted. Each mapped pin is an official route *start or end* coordinate. The XML supplies no intermediate road geometry, so the app draws no road line and does not claim the whole affected section lies on a straight connector. The list shows source report/update times separately from retrieval time, category and region filters, source link and uncertainty note.

The feed contains **no cameras**, camera snapshot rights, or image URLs. The app links to [official SD511](https://www.sd511.org/) for camera viewing and does not proxy or reproduce images. Do not infer that an absent report means a road is safe or open. For travel decisions, check the official map and road signs.

Reuse basis for local informational viewing: the feed is identified by the transportation exchange study as state-provided to the public. [South Dakota's online disclaimer](https://www.sd.gov/bit?id=sdrs_kb_article_view&spa=1&sys_kb_id=d9449aa41b8c811062c8a8eae54bcb9f&sysparm_article=KB0010221) allows viewing, copying and distribution for personal or informational use, while warning that materials may carry third-party rights. This project does not claim a general commercial redistribution license, rehost the ZIP, or copy camera images.
